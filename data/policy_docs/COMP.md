# Comprehensive (COMP)

Policy wording for the Comprehensive motor product. Your schedule shows which covers you bought and the limit and deductible for each one.

## COMP-COV-COLLISION | Collision cover
- type: coverage
- perils: collision

We will pay for physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision or impact with another vehicle, a person, an animal or a fixed or moving object, including hitting a wall, pole, kerb or gate.

## COMP-COV-VANDALISM | Vandalism cover
- type: coverage
- perils: vandalism

We will pay for malicious damage to the insured vehicle caused deliberately by someone other than you or a driver of the vehicle, such as keying, scratching, kicked panels or smashed glass.

## COMP-COV-WEATHER | Weather cover
- type: coverage
- perils: weather

We will pay for damage to the insured vehicle caused by storm, hail, windstorm or objects falling on the vehicle during a storm.

## COMP-COV-THEFT | Theft cover
- type: coverage
- perils: theft

We will pay for loss of the insured vehicle, or damage to it, caused by theft or attempted theft.

## COMP-DED | Deductible
- type: deductible

For each claim we take off the deductible shown in your schedule for the cover that applies. If the repair costs less than the deductible, we pay nothing.

## COMP-LIM | Limit of cover
- type: limit

The most we will pay for any one claim is the limit shown in your schedule for the cover that applies.

## COMP-EXC-INTENTIONAL_DAMAGE | Intentional damage
- type: exclusion
- excludes: intentional_act

We will not pay for loss or damage caused on purpose by you, a member of your household or any person driving the vehicle with your permission.

## COMP-EXC-PRE_EXISTING_DAMAGE | Pre-existing damage
- type: exclusion
- excludes: damage_before_policy

We will not pay for damage that existed before the start date of this policy or before the incident you are claiming for.

## COMP-EXC-UNLICENSED_DRIVER | Unlicensed driver
- type: exclusion
- excludes: unlicensed_driver

We will not pay for loss or damage while the vehicle is being driven by a person who does not hold a valid driving licence.

## COMP-EXC-DUI | Driving under the influence
- type: exclusion
- excludes: driver_impaired

We will not pay for loss or damage while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or is over the legal alcohol limit.

## COMP-EXC-RACING | Racing and track use
- type: exclusion
- excludes: racing_or_track_use

We will not pay for loss or damage while the vehicle is used for racing, pace-making, speed testing, rallies, track days or on any race circuit.

## COMP-CON-PERIOD | Period of insurance
- type: condition

Cover only applies to incidents that happen during the period of insurance shown in your schedule. We will not pay for incidents before the start date or after the end date.

## COMP-CON-REPORTING | Reporting a claim
- type: condition
- reporting_window_days: 30

You must report an incident to us within 30 days of the date it happened. If you report it later, we may refuse the claim.

## COMP-GEN-CLAIMS | How to make a claim
- type: general

Tell us what happened, send clear photos of the damage and a repair estimate. We may ask for a police report if another party was involved.

## COMP-GEN-CANCEL | Cancelling your policy
- type: general

You can cancel at any time. If no claim has been made, we refund the unused part of your premium.
